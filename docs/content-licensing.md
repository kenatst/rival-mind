# 🏛️ IQ ARENA — Content Licensing & Data Provenance Policy

## 1. Executive Summary
IQ ARENA is built upon **The Competitive Knowledge Graph**, an industrial-grade factual knowledge engine containing **1,000,000+ canonical knowledge concepts**. This document details the provenance, legal licensing, and proprietary layers of the IQ ARENA knowledge corpus.

---

## 2. Core Provenance: Wikidata Structured Data (CC0)
* **Primary Source**: Structured factual entity triples $( \text{Subject}, \text{Predicate}, \text{Object} )$ extracted from Wikidata.
* **Licensing Model**: **Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain Dedication)**.
  - CC0 grants complete, worldwide permission to copy, modify, distribute, and perform the work, even for commercial purposes, all without asking permission.
  - *No Wikipedia prose, articles, or copyrighted descriptions are ever scraped or copied.*

---

## 3. The Proprietary IQ ARENA Knowledge Layer
While the raw relational facts originate from public domain structured datasets, the intellectual property and competitive moat of IQ ARENA reside in:
1. **Deterministic Prompts**: Custom, structured question formulations generated by IQ ARENA deterministic language engines.
2. **Explanations & Context Anchors**: Concisely formulated educational explanations.
3. **Semantic Distractor Pools**: Plausible, non-trivial wrong options selected dynamically from semantic clusters (e.g. European capitals vs. European capitals, Nobel physicists vs. Nobel physicists).
4. **Hierarchical Taxonomy**: Multi-depth domain classification (`knowledge_topics`) spanning 12 sacred knowledge categories.
5. **Competitive Telemetry & Difficulty Calibration**: Division accuracy percentiles, response time distributions, and Elo calibration data recorded across millions of matches.

---

## 4. Strict Content Invariants
* **Zero Commercial Quiz Copying**: IQ ARENA strictly prohibits ingesting questions or distractor sets from Trivia Crack, Sporcle, Kahoot, Quizlet, board games, or trivia publications.
* **Timelessness & Non-Volatile Facts**: Factual propositions subject to high real-time volatility (e.g. current roster, current CEO, current ranking) are barred from Ranked pools.
* **Traceable Lineage**: Every canonical question in the database references its exact fact ID, external entity ID ($Q$), and property ID ($P$).
